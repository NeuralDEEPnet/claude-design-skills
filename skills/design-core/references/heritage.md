# Heritage — classics as working lenses
A lens, not a style. Roles: [evidence] [opinion] [trend] [inspiration] [inference]; Take lines, checks and carry-forward rows are [inference]. Ids (`WB BR SW BO AW PG PL OL` + claim id) are maintainers' provenance in `docs/heritage/verified-research.md`, not in the app skill: cite a row's topic. Other facts: `references/heritage-ledger.md`.

## Rapid card
Reply: at most 60 of the 400 words. (1) One strand line: phase or team, "inspired by", not a look. (2) That strand's trap as an anti-principle. (3) One check below, with its result: web or CAD = a measured value with its basis tag; image, motion or world = one yes/no rubric question [inference, simulated reviewer]. The declared set goes in the DNA as one line, outside the 60 words, each value with its basis tag: "unit 8 px [house heuristic] · type 1.25 [house heuristic] · 12 columns [brief]".
Two named: one line each inside the same 60 words. No strand card (Scandinavian, mid-century, Memphis, Apple, Muji): say once "no verified card for X", give a principle and a check [inference], no dates or attributions.
Checks: C1 name each element's function, remove one at a time, re-run the task. C2 declare unit and ratio, measure every value, list outliers. C3 view at three widths, in greyscale, in one colour, with user text overrides. C4a controls by tier, product at rest. C4b a reveal only for a named task: states and fallback. C5a differences from comparators, structural or surface. C5b award readiness: evidence status per criterion, never a verdict.
Rubrics: image: are edges and focal points on a declared line or listed exception? motion: is every transition on the declared ramp and easing? world: is every scale anchor sourced or [brief]?

## Operating rules
- Name the strand and phase (Bauhaus default: the Dessau 1925 programme; say it is an assumption). Take the principle, not the period device. Each reference gets a `Becomes` cell (`templates.md`): a DNA principle with a rejection example, an anti-principle, or a check below; else delete the row. A rejection example names an element in this deliverable that would change; if none could, delete the row.
- Say "inspired by", never "X style", and never claim that a person, company, school or award body approves or is affiliated. Naming the inspiration in your reply or a credit line is fine; leave it out of a client's product copy unless the client wants it. Living designers (Rams, Fukasawa, Morrison): take principles, do not imitate the person. Do not ship commercial fonts you have not licensed (Helvetica, Helvetica Neue, Arial); a fallback stack may name Helvetica.
- Dates, attributions, quotes, numbers and criteria only from a card or ledger row; else omit, or write an [inference] as a hypothesis with no date, number, quote or attribution.
- One accent, one family, two weights, the 8 px step: studio heuristics, not the classics'. Basis tags: [standard: <name>] [platform-fixed] [house heuristic] [brief]; [brief] = the user or client stated it, your own pick is [house heuristic].
- No scoring, no "P#" labels. Awards: never a score, odds or a word like ready, strong, likely. Asked to score against Rams' ten principles: decline numbers; per principle say in hand / to produce / not applicable. Standard: at most 4 reference rows; add a Carry forward row per kept principle ("none" is valid).

## Strands
### Werkbund–Bauhaus
Dessau 1925 programme (Gropius): form from a list of functions; typical forms; prototypes for industry; ornament only if functionally justified [evidence, WB c15-c18]. No single look or dogma [opinion, WB c23].
Take: list the functions before drawing; fix a few typical forms and state what may vary between items.
Trap: primary colours with geometric blocks; "Bauhaus = minimal".
Checks: C1 C2 C3

### Braun–Eichler–Rams
Braun's language was a team result: Eichler, Gugelot, Aicher, Wagenfeld, Hirche, Rams [evidence, BR c9 c11]. Atelier 1 and L1 share "the same proportions as the SK4" [evidence, BR av Atelier].
Take: each control serves a stated purpose; one layout order across a family; units that share proportions.
Trap: white box with orange accent; an invented grid (Moss 1962 gives none [evidence, BR c24 c25]).
Checks: C1 C2 C3 C4a

### HfG Ulm–Aicher
Ulm 1953-68 [evidence, BR c28 c31]. Munich 1972 pictograms on a vertical-and-diagonal grid [evidence, BR c33 c34]; a 2025 audit (second-hand) found uneven limb thickness [opinion, BR c35].
Take: a system with a method: declare the rules, then measure every instance.
Trap: a grid that only looks consistent.
Checks: C2 C3

### Swiss / International Typographic Style
Müller-Brockmann, Grid Systems 1981: field depth is a whole number of text lines; 57 lines in 4 fields: 4×13+3=55 [evidence, SW c2 c3].
Take: derive fields from a declared line pitch.
Trap: Swiss = Helvetica [SW c11]; crediting him a baseline grid, a two-weight cap [SW c6-c8] or golden modules [SW c4].
Checks: C2 C3

### Helvetica
Neue Haas Grotesk 1957, renamed Helvetica c.1959-61 [evidence, SW c18]; commercial, not redistributable [SW c21]. "Neutral" (MoMA 2007) is disputed [opinion, SW c23 c26 c27].
Take: choose a face for a stated job; open stack: Inter (OFL) [SW c28]; Arimo and Liberation are Arial-metric [SW c29].
Trap: calling it neutral; bundling it.
Checks: C3

### Bang & Olufsen
Counter-evidence first: iFixit rated Beoplay Eleven 1/10 [evidence, BO c30]; Beosound 9000 faults rest on one enthusiast source [BO c19 c20].
Take: controls by tier; the product at rest still reads; a reveal only for a named task.
Trap: ceremony as decoration; "B&O is Bauhaus" is company or commentator framing [opinion, BO c8].
Checks: C4a C4b C1

### iF / Red Dot / Good Design
Criteria are vocabulary, not authority: jury judgments on paid, self-selected entries [evidence, AW c06 c09 c35 c36]; a 768-criteria scoring system (1977) was unused from 1983 [evidence, AW av Ohl].
Take: stamp the edition of the criteria (ledger crosswalk); plan the evidence the schemes do not test.
Trap: scores; promising a win.
Checks: C5b C5a

### Proportion systems
Declared families: √2 / ISO 216 [PG c9-c11], ISO 3 preferred numbers [PG c15-c17], Modulor [PG c12-c14]; tables in `references/proportion.md`.
Take: declare one family; measure with C2.
Trap: the Parthenon measures about 2.25, not 1.618 [PG c4]; R10 is 1.259, not 1.25 [PG c15-c17].
Checks: C2

### De Stijl–Constructivism
De Stijl manifesto, Nov 1918: balance of the universal and the individual; no palette or angle rule [evidence, OL ds1 ds2]. Constructivist graphics show at least three formal modes [inference, OL cv2-cv5].
Take: a declared vocabulary of few shapes, hues and angles; mirror the layout: does it still balance?
Trap: "primaries on a grid"; "diagonals = Constructivism".
Checks: C2 C1

### Olivetti–Vignelli
Olivetti's 1954 Compasso d'Oro jury cited "stylistic coherence" from graphics to shops [opinion, company archive, OL ol1-ol4]. Vignelli's Canon (2010): he works with about half a dozen typefaces, maybe twice that [opinion, OL vg1-vg3].
Take: one system across touchpoints through shared tokens.
Trap: a per-project cap of two typefaces.
Checks: C2 C3

### Klint–Super Normal–Muji–Eames
Klint measured human dimensions and started from existing types [evidence, OL kl1]. Super Normal (2007) defines the quality through use [opinion, OL jp2 jp3]. Eames leg splint 1941-42: holes relieve stress, the shape stacks for shipping [evidence, OL us1].
Take: record each body-contact dimension's quantity, source and tolerance; name the existing product each control borrows from; one row per form decision: constraint → form.
Trap: "Super Normal = Muji look"; unsourced slogans.
Checks: C2 C4a C1

## Checks
Each: test · fails when. Numbers are [house heuristic] unless tagged; tunables are declared with their basis; none is the classics' doctrine.

C1 Purpose ledger, then remove one at a time. Test: write each element's function BEFORE removing it; remove elements singly and re-run the primary task; with text labels hidden, a fresh reader names the function and primary action [inference, simulated reviewer; with no reader write "not tested"]. Elements the client's brand or design system defines are listed as client identity, not removed. Fails when: an element stays on a post-hoc "brand" reason, or a kept element changes nothing when removed.

C2 Declared set, then measure. Test: write the set before the build (block below), then measure every value: web tokens; image grid and crops; 3D dimension and radius families; motion duration ramp and easing family; world scale anchors. Report "N outliers of M values". [platform-fixed] values are exempt (at most 3, each naming its platform rule); u ≥ 4 px unless platform-fixed; change u only as a logged revision. Client-defined tokens are measured and reported, never changed without the client. A named ratio uses its true value (R10 = 1.259); any other value is called by its own number; within 1% passes only a measured ratio. Fails when: an outlier has no named function or fix, or a ratio claim is over 1% off unqualified.

C3 Reproduction loss. Test: view at 320 px [standard: WCAG 1.4.10] and two other widths, in greyscale, in one colour or print, at the final crops, in light and dark, and with the user's text overrides (line height 1.5, paragraph spacing 2×, letter spacing 0.12 em, word spacing 0.16 em [standard: WCAG 1.4.12]). Fails when: meaning or the primary action is lost, a state relies on colour alone, or an override clips or overlaps content (fixed-height boxes and tight leading are the usual causes).

C4a Controls by tier, product at rest. Test: name the top 3 tasks; mark each control primary or secondary; no primary control is hidden; a hidden control gets a visible cue and a path that needs no motion; view the product with every light and display off and name the alignment lines that remain (edges or gaps that line up). Fails when: a primary control is hidden, a hidden one lacks a cue or path, or no alignment line can be named.

C4b Reveal and repair evidence. A reveal is a lid, drawer or moving part that hides or shows controls. Default: none. Test: remove the motion; if no named task needs it, drop it. Kept: specify closed, moving, open, blocked and power-lost states, the response to an obstruction and a manual fallback. Repair: list parts, fasteners, procedure and availability period, or write "unknown". Fails when: the reveal serves no task, a state or the fallback is missing, or repairability is claimed without evidence.

C5a Differentiation by structure. Test: a comparator table with URLs; tag each difference structural (changes what it does or how it is built) or surface (only how it looks); a second reviewer confirms [inference, simulated reviewer]. Fails when: every difference is surface, or a comparator has no URL.

C5b Award readiness. Test: stamp the edition of the criteria (if the user's edition differs, state the stamped one and mark the other unverified); ask first what evidence exists (CAD, prototype, tests, certificates, accessibility review); per criterion say in hand / to produce / not applicable; name what the schemes do not test (user evidence, accessibility conformance, performance, safety certification [inference, AW c35 c36]) and plan each; safety conformity is the maker's duty; no invented research: offer a labelled template. Close with the open items and the first one to produce, never a verdict. Fails when: a score, odds, percentage, criteria count or verdict word (ready, strong, likely, contender) appears, or evidence is invented.

Passing shows self-consistency with the declared rules, not quality or lineage authenticity.

## Tensions
Surface only live tensions: Rapid none (take the default silently); Standard at most 3; Full as needed. State each as collision → default taken → what would reverse it; only a fork that blocks the build is asked, and it uses one of the 3 questions. Rows: collision | default | reverses when.
- Client identity vs named tradition | client wins; the tradition gives principles | the client relaxes it
- Standardisation vs individuality | types plus a stated variation space [WB c16-c18] | the brief needs a one-off
- Neutrality vs warmth | choose face and material for the job; never "neutral" | the brief wants warmth or a clinical register
- Reveal vs restraint | no reveal (C4b) | a named task needs it
- Award criteria vs evidence | plan the evidence; claim nothing | the evidence exists
- Economy vs accessibility | economy = fewer decisions, never fewer functions | a tested need shows a cue is redundant
- Disputed dates | give the range with sources | one date is needed: name its source

Settled by fact: Sullivan coined "form follows function", 1896 [WB c20]; Bauhaus had no single look or dogma; the Parthenon measures about 2.25, not 1.618; the 1970 NYC manual specified Standard Medium, not Helvetica [SW c24]. At Full a tension can be the divergence axis: two concepts at its poles, one refusing it.

## Declared set
Write it before the build, in the artifact (CSS comment or spec block); arithmetic and tables: `references/proportion.md`.
```
unit u:       _ px (web) | _ mm (object); u ≥ 4 px unless platform-fixed
ratio family: √2 | R10 1.259 | _ ; type ratio _, or sizes at 320/1280 px
grid/module:  columns × gutter × line pitch L | module _ mm
radius/angle: _ ; hues: _ + neutrals
exempt:       at most 3, each with its rule, e.g. 44 pt native iOS control [platform-fixed, PG c24]
thresholds:   ratio ±1%; type ±0.5 px; object ±_ mm
every value carries a basis tag
```
Residue = `min(v mod u, u − v mod u)` for padding, margin, gap, box sizes, radii and line-height (objects: every mm dimension). After the build list outliers: value · measured · function or fix. Necessary, not sufficient. Full: change u and list the values that did not move.
