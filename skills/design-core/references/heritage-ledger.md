## Award crosswalk

Edition-stamped; criteria change each cycle. Quote that edition's wording; re-check the organiser page. Store no fees, weights or officers.

- **iF Design Award, 2025 award onward** (organiser release 29 Oct 2024; ifdesign.com blocked reads): Idea, Form, Function, Differentiation, Sustainability. Sustainability replaced Impact (2021-24). Equal weighting is organiser-reported. AW c03, c05, c07 https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/if-design-award-announces-new-sustainability-criteria-2025-final-deadline-approaching-on-november-6th-302290089.html
- **Red Dot Product Design 2026** (Guide to Success, Oct 2025): degree of innovation, ergonomics, product periphery, functionality, durability, self-explanatory quality, formal quality, ecological compatibility, symbolic and emotional content. No weights published; jurors set emphasis. 2008 wording differed. AW c14, c15, c17-c19 https://www.red-dot.org/fileadmin/downloads/Downloadmaterial_PD_2025/Guide_to_Success_EN.pdf

Not tested by the schemes in the documents read `[inference]`: user evidence, accessibility conformance, performance, safety certification (AW c35, c36). Safety and electrical conformity are the maker's duty. Make each a plan item: in hand / to produce / not applicable; no scores, no promise of a win. Awards are jury judgments on paid, self-selected entries; exhibitions (Die gute Form 1949, MoMA Good Design 1950-55) are not awards (AW c25-c27).
