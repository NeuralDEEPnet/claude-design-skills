# Verified research: design heritage and the 2026 platform

Audit trail for `skills/design-core/references/heritage-ledger.md`. The ledger's ids point into this file (`WB c20` = §1, claim c20; `BR`=§2, `SW`=§3, `BO`=§4, `AW`=§5, `PG`=§6, `PL`=§7, `OL`=§8). It is not loaded by any skill at runtime.

Method. On 2026-11-06 eight strands were researched from primary sources where reachable, then each strand was re-checked by an independent fact-checker whose brief was to refute it. Claims are kept (verified or corrected), dropped (unsupported or unsafe), or listed as "not carried" (verified but omitted for length: not part of the fact base unless re-verified). Some sites blocked reads (ifdesign.com, parts of moma.org and others): those gaps are named in each strand. To add or change a claim: record the source URL, its date and the role tag, and have it checked by someone other than its author.

Conventions. Roles: evidence, opinion (attributed), inspiration, trend, inference (agent-derived: not sourced; every numeric threshold in a check is a tunable heuristic, never the classics' doctrine). Company, museum and organiser pages are self-description, not independent evidence. Quotes stay under 15 words. Names (Braun, Vitsœ, Rams, B&O, iF, Red Dot, MoMA) are third-party marks: "inspired by", never endorsement or affiliation. Volatile facts (§10) must be re-checked before citing.

Pitfalls: Werkbund is not a Bauhaus offshoot and Muthesius did not "found" it in person. 1919 manifesto is craft-utopian, not industrial; the pivot is 1923/1925. "Form follows function" is Sullivan. No single Bauhaus style, and phases (Itten/Weimar, Gropius prototype programme, Meyer, Mies) differ. Not every icon was mass-produced (lamp, MT 49 silver-and-ebony student piece, B3). Tecnolumen re-editions are sold as 1924 originals; Wagenfeld credit varies (Wagenfeld; +Jucker; +Pap).

Checks (all [inference]; numbers are heuristics, not Bauhaus doctrine; only web/CAD thresholds are crisp, other media need a named reviewer rubric):
- [inference] Remove-one ablation: remove each element, re-run the brief's task; any element kept must name a function in the brief beforehand (a post-hoc "brand" excuse fails).
- [inference] Reproduction-loss test (best grounded, c18): render under three widths, greyscale, print/one-colour; for CAD, name the process and its rules; pass = stated meaning survives, measured by a written rubric.
- [inference] Declared inventory + module audit: state limits and module up front (default for unbranded: <=3 base forms, <=3 hues + neutrals, <=2 type families; >=90% of values on the module); outliers need a stated function. Tunable, never "Bauhaus rules".
- [inference] Type-plus-variation: name the type (constants) and the variation space; build 3 instances that share constants and differ only in content-driven attributes.
- [inference] Colour in context: test each key colour on two grounds with a named metric and threshold (WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.3 4.5:1/3:1 for text; delta E for others); state not carried by colour alone.

## 9. Convergences and tensions across strands

Where the classics agree (each supported by claims above):
- Honesty and anti-ornament: Gropius 1925 (ornament only if functionally justified; machine goods need their own authenticity), Rams/Vitsœ "honest", MoMA Good Design "truth to materials", Red Dot "self-explanatory quality", Eames' constraint-to-form ledger, Rand "integrity, honesty". The shared test: can each form decision cite a function or constraint?
- Economy: Gropius "economical use of space, material, time, money", Albers' minimal material/energy/time, Eichler/Rams "as little design as possible", Vignelli's few typefaces, ISO 17 "coarsest series that works". Economy means fewer decisions, not fewer functions.
- System over object: Werkbund Typisierung, Gropius types with variation space, Eichler's family coherence, Braun units "in the same proportions as the SK4", Müller-Brockmann's grid, Olivetti's stylistic coherence, Renard/ISO series, Vitsœ 606, M 125, Aicher's rules, Vignelli's canon.
- Declare the rule set, then measure: Müller-Brockmann's line arithmetic, Klint's measured bodies, Renard series, Moss's alignments. Where a source gives no number, no number is attributable to it.
- Anti-dogma and self-revision: Gropius 1968 (no style, no prescription), Rams (principles open to revision), Tschichold's retreat, Müller-Brockmann (grid "no guarantee of success"), Rand (rules differ each time).
- Real production and real use: Gropius on quality lost between model and copy, Eames' shipping and stress, Red Dot's live physical judging, Klint/Modulor/ADA human anchors.

Where they genuinely conflict:
- Standardisation vs individuality: Muthesius vs van de Velde 1914 (Edelmann: unresolved; Gropius 1925 endorses types with room for personal variation); Müller-Brockmann's "will to objectivity" vs "the character of the designer shows".
- Swiss neutrality vs expressive material warmth: Helvetica "neutral" (MoMA) vs disputed (Spiekermann, Experimental Jetset, Matteson); Müller-Brockmann's reduction vs B&O rosewood/teak/aluminium finishes, Aalto's chosen warmth, Klint's costly woods.
- Rams' restraint vs B&O ceremony/reveal: "unobtrusive", "as little design as possible" vs motorised lids and doors that open on approach; B&O's own record (Beosound 9000 service faults) shows reveals add failure modes.
- Awards vs user evidence: criteria are jury judgments on paid, self-selected entries; no scheme read requires user studies, accessibility audits or safety evidence; MoMA's curators call Good Design "elitist and crassly commercial - and not without cause"; Ohl's 768-criteria system (1977) was dropped by 1983.
- Craft vs industry: Weimar craft-utopia vs Dessau prototypes; Gropius allows handmade prototypes, yet the Wagenfeld lamp at 55 RM and the B3 chair were not mass-market. Ulm's scientific method vs Bill's "good form".
- Proportion myth vs practice: golden-ratio claims fail measurement (Parthenon 2.25), yet declared ratio families (sqrt2, Renard, Modulor-style) work as rule sets.
- Reduction vs accessibility: tight print leading, hidden secondary controls and label-free ideals collide with WCAG 1.4.12/2.5.8 and discoverability.
- Heritage restraint vs 2026 novelty: a feature being newly available is not a reason to use it.

Surface to the user, do not resolve silently: (1) client identity or ornament vs the house style; (2) standardisation vs individuality when a brief needs both; (3) neutrality vs warmth; (4) reveal/ceremony vs restraint, and whether the reveal serves a task; (5) award criteria vs evidence, and fee/selection effects; (6) economy vs accessibility, discoverability or legibility; (7) disputed dates and attributions (give ranges with sources). An agent MAY resolve, by fact, those the verified record settles (golden ratio claims, "form follows function" attribution, Helvetica/subway, Bauhaus style).
