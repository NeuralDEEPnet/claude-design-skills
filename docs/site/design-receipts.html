Why now

AI design tools are everywhere, and so is the question that follows every output: can you defend it? Clients ask how work was made. The European Accessibility Act has applied since June 2025. The EU AI Act's transparency duties took effect in August 2026. Generators answer none of that. A receipt does.

What a Design Receipt is

A short, structured record that travels with the deliverable. Same shape for a landing page, a poster, a printed part, a film, or a virtual world.

  • Checks with numbers. Contrast ratios, target sizes, wall thickness, frame time: method, result, threshold, pass or fail.
  • Decisions with traces. Each important choice points to a user need, a constraint, or a design principle, with a confidence label: measured, observed, inferred, or assumed.
  • The not-done list. What was not rendered, measured, or tested, and what to run instead. Always present. Never empty.
  • AI use, in plain words. Which steps were AI-generated, AI-assisted, or human-made, and who reviewed them.
  • One next test. The single validation that would most change the verdict.
